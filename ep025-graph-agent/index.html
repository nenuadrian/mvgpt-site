AI agent: a graph database and a calculator

Our agent gets a fourth role, which queries smollerGraph in Cypher, so it can take two facts from a graph database and combine them with the calculator, and we measure what it kept of its old tasks.

Watch on YouTube

In this episode, our agent gets a fourth role. Next to the calculator and the files, it can now hand a question to a subagent that writes a Cypher query for smollerGraph, the graph database we build from scratch in its own series. So asked how much heavier the king penguin is than the great white pelican, it asks the graph twice and the calculator once. We generate forty-five thousand examples, almost half of them the agent's old tasks, fine-tune episode twenty-four's model for thirty minutes on the laptop, and measure the new tasks, the graph role on its own, and what the agent kept of its old tasks.
