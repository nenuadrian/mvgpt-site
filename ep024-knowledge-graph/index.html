Text to Cypher: our GPT asks a graph database

We fine-tune our agent to write Cypher queries for smollerGraph, the graph database built in our sibling series, and to answer from what comes back, then measure it with the graph and without.

Watch on YouTube

Facts trained into a model's weights take training again to change, so in this episode our model learns to look them up. smollerGraph, the graph database we build from scratch in its own series, holds the mammals and birds on Wikidata, and our agent learns to write a Cypher query, send it to the database and answer from what comes back. We generate thirty thousand questions, most answered by the graph, fine-tune for twenty minutes on the laptop, and measure it on species training doesn't ask about, with the graph and without.
