    Text diffusion: a GPT that writes all at once

    We turn our GPT into a masked diffusion model, which writes a story by unhiding it, train it on the laptop and on four rented GPUs, start one from our next-token models' weights, and measure it against next-token prediction, comparing our code with LLaDA's, which trains this recipe at 8 billion parameters.

    Watch on YouTube

    This time, our GPT becomes a masked diffusion model, which writes a story by starting from a window of hidden tokens and unhiding the ones it's surest of, anywhere in the story. Then we measure it against next-token prediction, fill in the middle of stories, and start a diffusion model from a next-token model's weights, as Dream 7B did.
