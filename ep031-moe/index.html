    Mixture of experts from scratch in PyTorch

    We replace each block's MLP in our GPT with a mixture of eight experts and a router, write it as a loop and then as one batched multiply, balance it three ways, and train it next to the dense model at the same compute per token.

    Watch on YouTube

    In this episode, we give our GPT a mixture of experts: eight smaller MLPs in each block, and a router that sends each token to two of them. We write it as a loop over the experts, measure it, and then run all the experts in one batched matrix multiply. We balance the experts with Switch Transformer's loss and with DeepSeek-V3's bias, see what happens without either, train each for two thousand steps next to the dense model on this Mac, and look at which words each expert ends up with.

    How a mixture of experts works
