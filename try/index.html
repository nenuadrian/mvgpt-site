    Try mvGPT

    Both models run in this page, on your own computer, with Transformers.js. Nothing downloads until the first story, and what you type never leaves the page.

    Talk to mvGPT

    ep003-sft · 8-bit · in your browser
      Features:

      A summary, or a few words to use (sun, kite, brave), and it writes a children's story. It doesn't answer questions, and each message is a new story. The first one downloads the model, about 15 MB, from Hugging Face.

      The beginning of a story, and it carries on. Six times the size, it trained for 30 minutes on four GPUs, on all of TinyStories, and wasn't fine-tuned, so it doesn't follow instructions. The first story downloads the model, about 92 MB, from Hugging Face.

      Where they come from

      14.6M, fine-tuned
      Pretrained on TinyStories, then fine-tuned to write the story an instruction asks for, in episode 3. It's given a summary or a few words, and the features to put in.
      91M, pretrained on 4 GPUs
      Six times the size, trained for 30 minutes on four GPUs on all of TinyStories, in episode 12. It wasn't fine-tuned, so it carries on whatever it's given.

      Both are on Hugging Face, as 8-bit ONNX exports of the checkpoints the episodes trained, and the code that trained them is on GitHub.
