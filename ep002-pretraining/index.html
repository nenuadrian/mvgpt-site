    Pretraining a GPT from scratch in PyTorch

    From random tokens to short stories in a first training run on a laptop, with a tokenizer, the next-token objective, a training loop and evaluation written from scratch.

    Watch on YouTube

    Today, we pretrain our GPT on TinyStories, starting with a word-level tokenizer and the next-token objective, with cross-entropy as the loss. We write the training loop, with AdamW, a learning rate that warms up and then follows a cosine, and gradient clipping, and train for ten minutes on a laptop. Then we write stories with the model and with an early checkpoint, and measure the checkpoints on held-out stories, from the loss and perplexity to the likeliest next tokens.
