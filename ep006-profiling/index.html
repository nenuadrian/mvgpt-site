    Profiling a GPT with PyTorch's profiler

    We look at our model as a graph of operations in Model Explorer, profile it with PyTorch's profiler, and measure how decisions in its code change its speed on a CPU and a GPU.

    Watch on YouTube

    In this episode, we look at where our model's time goes, starting with a graph of its 163 operations in Google's Model Explorer, exported with torch.export. Then PyTorch's profiler times a full pass and the writing of a token at a time, on the CPU and on the GPU. And we measure a few decisions in the code, such as our GELU written out, torch.compile and the size of the vocabulary, as each changes the speed by a different amount on each device.
