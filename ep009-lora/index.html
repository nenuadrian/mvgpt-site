LoRA: fine-tuning a GPT with 2% of its weights

We fine-tune our model on instructions again with LoRA, which freezes its weights and trains a small low-rank update beside them, and measure what it saves and what it learns against full fine-tuning.

Watch on YouTube

We're going to fine-tune with low-rank adaptation, LoRA, which freezes the pretrained weights and trains a small update beside each linear layer, as the product of two thin matrices. We write it in PyTorch, fine-tune on instructions for ten minutes with about 2% of the weights, and merge the update back into the model. Then we compare it with full fine-tuning from episode three, on the file we keep, the optimiser's memory, the losses and the stories.
