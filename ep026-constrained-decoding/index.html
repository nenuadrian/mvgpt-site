    Constrained decoding: structured LLM output

    Nothing is trained. Before each token inside a query's name, our agent's graph role masks the tokens that would take the name off a list, and we measure two lists, the graph's names and the task's words.

    Watch on YouTube

    In this episode, our agent stops misspelling the okapi, with no training at all. Last time, its graph role copied the name wrong into its Cypher queries, and smollerGraph found nothing. Today, before each token inside a query's name, the tokens that would take the name off a list get a logit of minus infinity, which is how structured outputs keep a model to a JSON schema. We build it from scratch, with a trie of the vocabulary's tokens walked together with a trie of the names, a byte at a time. The obvious list, the names the graph has, turns the okapi into the oak titmouse, a real name and the wrong animal. The second list, the words of the task, gets it right, and we measure both on the graph role's held-out questions, and the task's words on the agent's tasks.
