Pretraining on the web: a GPT overnight

We pretrain a model in GPT-2 small's shape on educational web pages for a whole night, on one RTX 4090 rented for under four dollars, and measure it against GPT-2 small on HellaSwag.

Watch on YouTube

In this episode, we move our model from children's stories to the web, with FineWeb-Edu, pages that a classifier scored as educational, and BPE merges learned on them. We work out how many tokens a night on one GPU can buy, against the Chinchilla paper's twenty tokens per parameter, and train a model of about 110 million parameters for nearly eight hours on a rented RTX 4090. In the morning, we measure it on HellaSwag next to GPT-2 small, and in bits per character on stories and on web pages.
