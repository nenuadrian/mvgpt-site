    LLM tool calling from scratch: a calculator

    We fine-tune the model we pretrained on the web to call a calculator, in plain text, and measure how often it calls it right, on questions asked the way training asks and in new ways, with the calculator and without it.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our web model writes fluent text but can't do arithmetic, so today we teach it to call a calculator, as Toolformer did, writing the call as plain text for the program running it to fill in. We generate fifty thousand questions and fine-tune for ten minutes on the laptop, with the questions and the calculator's results left out of the loss. Then we measure the calls on held-out questions, worded the way training words them and in new ways, with the calculator and without it.
