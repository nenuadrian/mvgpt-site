    KV cache from scratch in PyTorch

    We add a KV cache to our model, so it runs each new token on its own and not the whole story again, and we check and time it on the CPU and the GPU.

    Watch on YouTube

    Our model runs the whole story through all its blocks for each token it writes, so this time we give it a KV cache, which keeps the keys and values of the earlier tokens. We add it to attention, with the positions and the mask it needs, and check that it changes nothing, with matching logits and stories. On a laptop, it writes about four times faster on the CPU, and less so on the GPU, and we finish with what a KV cache costs at the scale of Llama 3.1.
