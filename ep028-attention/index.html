    Attention heads in our GPT, seen and tested

    We look inside our agent while it copies a name into a query, with each head's attention written out, the logit lens, and a page of our own, then turn the heads that look at the name off to see what they do.

    Watch on YouTube

    In this episode, we look inside our GPT while its graph role copies a name from its task into a Cypher query. We write attention out by hand to keep each head's weights, put the logit lens on the residual stream to see each layer's guess at the next token, and build a page of our own to look at all 144 heads on a prompt. A head in the last layer puts 92 percent of its attention on the name in the task, and the name shows up in the last layer alone. For the okapi, that head looks at the right token, and the model can't write it inside the quotes. Then we turn the heads that look at the name off, against random ones, and find that they do more than copy.
