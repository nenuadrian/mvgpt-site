    GRPO: reinforcement learning without a critic

    Our model writes eight stories per instruction, and each is scored against the others, in place of PPO's critic, with group relative policy optimisation, the reinforcement learning behind DeepSeek-R1.

    Watch on YouTube

    Today, we write group relative policy optimisation, GRPO, the method behind DeepSeekMath and DeepSeek-R1, which does reinforcement learning without the critic our PPO needed. The model writes eight stories per instruction, and each story's advantage is its reward measured against the group's mean. We train for ten minutes with a KL penalty and without one, on episode eight's reward, and measure both against the fine-tuned model and PPO.
