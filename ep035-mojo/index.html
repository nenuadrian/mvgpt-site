    Our GPT's own GPU kernels in Mojo on a Mac

    We write our GPT's writing path as GPU kernels in Mojo, call them from Python, check them against PyTorch, and make them faster in three versions, racing PyTorch and MLX on this Mac.

    Watch on YouTube

    In this episode, we write the part of our GPT that writes text, a token at a time, as GPU kernels in Mojo, Modular's Python-like language, and call them from Python on this Mac's GPU. We check that they give PyTorch's logits and write PyTorch's story, then race them against PyTorch and MLX. The first version, a thread for each number, is the slowest of the three. Warps of 32 threads that read side by side, and then fewer kernels a token, after measuring what a kernel launch costs, bring it most of the way to MLX.
