    Understand language models from the ground up.

    Define a GPT in PyTorch, then take it through the whole training pipeline, from pretraining to reinforcement learning, with the evaluation and metrics that show what it learned, and deploy it for anyone to talk to.

    Talk to mvGPT

    ep003-sft · 8-bit · in your browser
      Features:

      A summary, or a few words to use (sun, kite, brave), and it writes a children's story. It doesn't answer questions, and each message is a new story. The first one downloads the model, about 15 MB, from Hugging Face.

      The beginning of a story, and it carries on. Six times the size, it trained for 30 minutes on four GPUs, on all of TinyStories, and wasn't fine-tuned, so it doesn't follow instructions. The first story downloads the model, about 92 MB, from Hugging Face.

      1. Our GPT's own GPU kernels in Mojo on a Mac

        We write our GPT's writing path as GPU kernels in Mojo, call them from Python, check them against PyTorch, and make them faster in three versions, racing PyTorch and MLX on this Mac.

        37 min

      2. Sparse autoencoder features inside our GPT

        We train a sparse autoencoder on the residual stream of our web model, read what its features fire on, measure what it costs the model, and steer the model's writing with a feature.

        26 min

      3. Our GPT in Apple's MLX vs PyTorch on a Mac

        We port our GPT from PyTorch to MLX, Apple's array library for its own chips, check that it gives the same numbers, and race the two on this Mac at writing and at training.

        24 min

      4. Mixture of experts from scratch in PyTorch

        We replace each block's MLP in our GPT with a mixture of eight experts and a router, write it as a loop and then as one batched multiply, balance it three ways, and train it next to the dense model at the same compute per token.

        37 min
